# V2.1 — summary of changes (from V2)

V1 (`../v1/`) and V2 (`../v2/`) are untouched. V2.1 is a copy of V2, improved rather than rebuilt.

## 1. A full ingredient cabinet (still at the top, still max 4)
- **42 ingredients** (up from 29). The 13 new ones each have their own drawing: eggplant, cucumber, peas, cream, tuna, yogurt, beans, tortilla, noodles, oats, blueberries, orange and peanut butter.
- Every shelf now holds an even number of ingredients and is drawn as two full rows: Vegetables 14, Protein & dairy 10, Carbs 8, Sweet & fruit 10. There are no empty gaps.
- On desktop, all 42 fit without scrolling. On a phone, the cabinet swipes sideways inside itself, and the tabs show which shelf you're looking at and jump between shelves.
- Ingredients press down when you tap them, and picked ones show a ✓.

## 2. Plate moved to its own serving area at the bottom
- The preparation area now holds only the 4 slots, the cutting board (bigger) and the knife.
- The new **serving area** is a wooden pass with the plate, a ticket, a fork and a service bell. It sits under the pot and oven and joins onto the stove.
- Small arrows link each stage: cabinet → prep → cooking → serving.
- The plate starts empty and inactive, and lights up ("serve here") only once the food is ready.

## 3. Real cooking moments
- **Picking:** the ingredient flies off the shelf into its slot, settles with a little bounce, and you hear the pick, whoosh and settle sounds.
- **Cutting:** you can grab the food anywhere on the board. The knife bobs when there's something to chop, the board bounces with each cut, and the pieces visibly separate.
- **Adding to the pot:** food falls in with a splash and a splash sound. If you used tap-to-place, it first flies over from its slot. A cracked egg drops in after its shell breaks.
- **Adding to the oven:** the door opens, and food slides onto the visible tray in order. The dial closes the door before baking starts.
- **The pot feels alive:** ingredients bob gently while simmering. The contents swirl along with your spoon as you stir, and the liquid takes on the colour of what's cooking and thickens once things are mixed. Steam, bubbles, melting, colour changes and overcooking smoke continue from V2. Changes in the food now fade smoothly instead of popping.
- **The oven:** the oven light comes on when baking or when the door is open, with a gentle heat glow and heat shimmer in the window. Food browns, melts and rises as it bakes, and a chime plays when it's ready. When the dish is done, the door wiggles and the window says "ready — open the door".

## 4. A real serving moment
- When the food is ready, the pot shows a **"ladle it out"** tag. The oven shows a **"take it out"** tag, but only once you open the door.
- To serve, drag the tag to the plate, or tap the tag and then tap the plate. Tapping the ready plate directly also works.
- The finished dish flies in an arc from the pot or oven to the plate, settles with a clink, and a little sparkle plays. **Only then** does the dish reveal screen appear, unchanged from V2.
- Serving raw food is refused ("not cooked yet"), unless you deliberately turned the heat off early. That still gives the "Lukewarm Hope" result.

## 5. Guidance without instructions
- One short hint line, plus a soft breathing outline on wherever to act next: the cabinet, the board, the stations, the knob, the oven door, or the plate.

## 6. Recipes
- **111 authored dishes** (up from 78): 82 familiar, 15 unusual, 14 surreal. The existing dishes are kept, and 33 new ones were added for the new ingredients. Examples: Ratatouille, Creamy Tomato Soup, Alfredo, Tuna Pasta, Creamy Curry, Ramen and Peanut Noodles, Porridge, Blueberry Jam, Marmalade, Tortilla Soup, Bean Chili and Pea Soup in the pot; Crème Brûlée, Eggplant Parmesan, Quesadilla, Tuna Melt and Tuna Pasta Bake, Fruit Crumble, Granola, Blueberry Muffins, Yogurt Cake and Baked Beans in the oven. There are also two new surprises: the Blueberry Galaxy and the Tortilla UFO.
- Every combination still gets a deterministic, ingredient-specific result through the improvisation system. Its name and plate are built from what you actually cooked.

## 7. Sound
- All V1 and V2 sounds are kept. Quiet new sounds were added in the same procedural style: whoosh (moving), settle, splash (dropping into the pot) and serve (plating clink). The mute button still works, and no sound plays before your first tap.

See `TEST_REPORT.md` §1 for the six real bugs fixed: early serving, serving from a closed oven, board grabbing, the cabinet mouse-drag, ungrabbable new picks and the recipe-step filter.
