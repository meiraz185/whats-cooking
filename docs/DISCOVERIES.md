# Discovery list — surprises & special discoveries

⚠️ Spoilers. Every surprise is authored and deterministic: repeating the same actions always reproduces it.

| Surprise | What you see | How to cause it |
|---|---|---|
| **The Charcoal Phoenix** | smoke bird rises from the ashes and flaps | Anything cooked into the Burnt phase. |
| **The Impossible Tower** | layers drop in one by one into a leaning tower | Oven: 4 ingredients, each added separately while baking (≥1 s apart). |
| **Onion Blossom** | petals unfold one by one | Oven: a whole (uncut) onion, alone or with one other ingredient. |
| **The Tiny Broccoli Forest** | pancake grows past the edges of the plate | Oven: a whole (uncut) broccoli, alone or with one other ingredient. |
| **Runaway Cheese Monster** | the food runs off the plate and back | Oven: cheese (+ at most one other) baked until Overcooked. |
| **Giant Strawberry Pancake** | pancake grows past the edges of the plate | Oven: flour + milk/egg stirred into batter + strawberry. |
| **Runaway Cloud Soufflé** | soufflé lifts off and hovers | Oven: egg cracked and stirred (whisked), with only dairy. |
| **Bubbling Mystery Stew** | eyes blink from inside the stew | Pot: 3+ ingredients mixing chocolate/fruit with vegetables, eggs, chicken, tofu or cheese. |
| **Chocolate Spaghetti Creature** | noodle creature wiggles on its stalk eyes | Pot: pasta + chocolate. |
| **Volcanic Mole** | sauce volcano erupts chocolate and tomato blobs | Pot: chopped tomato cooked to Ready, THEN chocolate. |
| **Popcorn Eruption** | sauce volcano erupts chocolate and tomato blobs | Pot: corn cooked until Overcooked. |
| **The Dough Blob** | noodle creature wiggles on its stalk eyes | Pot: pizza dough. |
| **The Blueberry Galaxy** | spin | Pot: blueberries + milk/cream/yogurt, stirred 4+ turns. |
| **The Tortilla UFO** | soufflé lifts off and hovers | Oven: a tortilla alone. |

Also unusual-but-plausible: Lukewarm Hope, Egg Drop Soup, Tangled Cheese-Rope Pasta, Sweet Fruit Pasta, Lumpy Dumpling Soup, Brownie Brick, Patchwork Flour Bake, Dessert Pizza, Crunchy Pasta Sticks, Strawberry & Cheese Toast, Chocolate Bread Bake, Dessert Noodles, Warm Tzatziki, Sweet Tortilla Roll-ups, Crispy Noodle Nest.
